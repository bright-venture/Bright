# Bright website

Static marketing site for Bright Service Company SARL (facility and support services, est. 2005), live at https://brightlb.com (Netlify; `www` redirects to the apex). Design and copy come from Kimi/ChatGPT exports; this repo prerenders them. Plain HTML/CSS/JS with a Node build step; no framework.

## Workflow

- Edit files in `src/` (and photos/logos/video in `source/`), then `npm run build`. The build writes the whole website into `site/`; commit it. Netlify publishes only `site/` (`netlify.toml`) and does not run a build, so nothing else in the repo is public.
- `npm run dev` builds, then serves at http://localhost:4321 (or the next free port). `scripts/serve.mjs` serves `site/` only, binds to 127.0.0.1, refuses paths outside it (checked with `path.relative` and `realpath`, never `startsWith`) and hidden files, and sends the same headers as `site/_headers`; keep it that way (Aikido SAST finding, Oct 2026). The preview config is `.claude/launch.json` (`bright-site`; it only serves, so build first).
- `npm run audit` runs Lighthouse (mobile + desktop) on every page in `sitemap.xml`; narrow it with language, device or page, e.g. `npm run audit -- ar mobile porter-services`. Target is 100 in every category. On this Windows machine headless Chrome occasionally stalls its compositor for ~1 to 2 s, giving a one-off 99; re-run or confirm on PageSpeed Insights.
- When a new Kimi export arrives: their `content.js` / `services-content.js` reintroduce wording we corrected. Merge only new or changed strings, keep ours otherwise, and re-check the Conventions below.

## Pages

- 18 pages: the homepage, four service pages and the contact page (`/contact/`) in `en` (`/`), `ar` (`/ar/`) and `el` (`/el/`), e.g. `/porter-services/`, `/ar/porter-services/`. Maintenance has no page; its card and links go to https://be-rightbright.com/.
- Each page has canonical, hreflang, Open Graph (a share image cropped from its hero photo) and JSON-LD (Organization, WebPage, plus Service and BreadcrumbList on service pages).

## Layout

- `src/layout.html`: shared document shell (header with brand, menu button, nav, language links; footer). Nav links point to `#section` on the homepage and to `/<lang>/#section` from service pages.
- `src/home.html`: homepage sections. `data-t="key"` replaces an element's text, `data-alt` an image alt, `data-aria` adds an `aria-label`, `data-photo="name"` expands to a responsive image. `<!--logos-->`, `<!--serviceCards-->`, `<!--values-->`, `<!--steps-->`, `<!--homePhotos-->` are filled by the build. Placeholder English text in templates is not used.
- `src/content.js`: `CONTENT` per locale (homepage text, service cards, values, steps, shared labels). `src/services-content.js`: `SERVICE_CONTENT` per locale (one entry per service page; an `externalUrl` entry gets no page). The build fails if a key is missing.
- `src/style.css`: Kimi's stylesheet, inlined at build time. Tokens on `:root` (`--navy #0b2038`, `--blue #006bd6`, `--cyan #36c7ff`). Latin and Greek use Arial; Arabic pages switch `--font` to the self-hosted Noto Sans Arabic. Our additions are marked with comments at the end (complete card rows).
- `src/app.js`: phone menu (below 680px, `.navigation.open`; styles scoped to `html.js` so links stay visible without JS), remembering the chosen language, and the video poster (set only near the viewport, since browsers fetch posters eagerly).
- `scripts/build.mjs`: renders all pages; photos to 480/640/800/1200/original px WebP, share images, logos, icons, the Arabic font subset, the video. Photos, share images, video and font files have content-hashed names (cached for a year via `netlify.toml`). It also writes `site/_headers`: the Content-Security-Policy, which allows the inline `<style>`/`<script>` blocks by SHA-256 hash of the final HTML. Never hand-edit built pages, and never add third-party scripts, fonts or embeds without adding them to the policy in `securityHeaders()`. `SITE`, `LANGS`, `LOGOS`, `HOME_PHOTOS`, `SIZES` live at the top.
- About section "Value of Agreement" chart: values and label positions in `AGREEMENT` (build.mjs), SVG generated by `agreementChart()` (monotone PCHIP curve), text keys `agreement*` in content.js, styles at the end of style.css, interaction in app.js. Add a year by adding an entry to `AGREEMENT`.
- `source/photos`, `source/logos`, `source/video`: originals. `source/video/bright-video.mp4` is a web encode (1080p H.264 ~2.2 Mbit/s, faststart, ~28 MB) of the 110 MB master from the 15 Oct 2026 export; keep masters out of git (GitHub rejects files over 100 MB) and re-encode with ffmpeg when the video changes. `source/unused`: photos not on the site (old gallery, maintenance photos).

## Conventions

- Never use em dashes in user-facing text (any language).
- Option buttons and tiles form even, complete grids, never ragged rows (the five service cards are 3 + 2 on desktop, 1 + 2 + 2 on tablet).
- When adding copy, add it to all three locales.
- Arabic: Western digits, "شركة برايت للخدمات ش.م.م." for the legal name, plural address (أعمالكم), porter service is "مساعدة المتسوقين" (never حمّال).
- Greek: sentence case for titles, porter service is "μεταφοράς αγορών" (never αχθοφόρος), motto "Χαρά μας να σας βοηθάμε".
- Contact details live in `CONTACT` at the top of `scripts/build.mjs` (contact page, footer, structured data): contact@brightlb.com (a Google Group; owners/members m.lamaa@ and s.choucair@brightlb.com, who reply as contact@ via Send mail as) and +961 79 300 350, +961 79 300 359, +961 1 488 450. Lebanese numbers drop the leading 0 after +961. Office: Q Business Center, Daoud Ammoun Street, Forn El Chebbak (address lines per language in `contactAddress`; coordinates and Google Maps/Waze links in `CONTACT`). No embedded map on purpose: a Google Maps iframe adds third-party scripts and cookies that break the CSP, privacy and the Lighthouse scores; the buttons open the map instead. Do not invent other details (hours, WhatsApp, floor) until the client provides them.
