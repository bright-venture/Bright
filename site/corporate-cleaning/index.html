OUR SERVICES

Corporate Cleaning & Specialized Deep Cleaning

Professional care for your premises. Consistent support for your operations.

Professional cleaning for supermarkets, shopping malls, offices and corporate facilities. From daily cleaning to specialized deep cleaning, our qualified teams provide floor and tile care, building exterior cleaning and high-level glass cleaning, with methods adapted to each site and surface.

Bright Service Company SARL provides corporate cleaning and specialized deep cleaning for commercial facilities across Lebanon. From supermarkets and shopping malls to offices and other business environments, we tailor our services to each facility’s activity, surfaces and operating hours.

With experience dating back to 2005, we combine qualified teams, professional equipment, organized schedules and regular follow-up to deliver consistent cleaning services.

Corporate Cleaning

Our routine cleaning services help maintain clean, presentable and welcoming environments for employees, customers and visitors.

The cleaning scope includes:

  • Floors, entrances, corridors and common areas
  • Offices, workspaces and meeting rooms
  • Washrooms and shared facilities
  • Interior glass and accessible windows
  • Frequently touched surfaces
  • Staff areas and back-of-house spaces

Tasks, staffing levels and cleaning frequency are defined according to each site’s requirements.

Supermarket Cleaning & Fresh Food Area Hygiene

Our extensive supermarket cleaning experience gives us a clear understanding of the particular hygiene requirements of fresh food departments, including produce, meat, poultry, seafood, deli and bakery areas.

These environments require special attention to food residues, spills and frequent activity. Working closely with store management, we coordinate cleaning routines with each department’s hygiene procedures and operational schedule.

Within the agreed scope, particular attention is given to floors, drains, work surfaces and designated equipment. Suitable cleaning products and dedicated tools help prevent cross-contamination, while cleaning activities are coordinated with food preparation, customer service and daily operations.

Specialized Deep Cleaning

Our qualified teams carry out intensive cleaning for surfaces and areas that require attention beyond the daily routine. Services are scheduled periodically or arranged for specific site requirements.

Specialist Floor & Tile Cleaning

Deep cleaning for different floor and tile surfaces, using equipment, products and techniques selected according to the material and its condition. Our scope includes machine-assisted cleaning where suitable, detailed tile and grout cleaning, and attention to edges, corners and difficult areas.

Building Exterior & Façade Cleaning

Cleaning of external walls and building façades to remove accumulated dirt and improve their appearance. Methods are selected following an assessment of the surface, its condition and access requirements.

High-Level Glass & Window Cleaning

Specialized cleaning of elevated windows, glass façades and other high-level glazing. Our teams plan each assignment around the site’s access requirements, using suitable equipment and working methods.

Detailed Cleaning of Fixtures & Difficult Areas

Targeted cleaning of fixtures, fittings and areas that are difficult to cover during routine cleaning. Tasks and access arrangements are agreed following a site assessment.

Opening & Post-Renovation Cleaning

Cleaning support to prepare premises for opening or returning to use following renovation, layout changes or other works. The scope is adapted to the condition of the site and the work completed.

Professional Tools & Equipment

We place strong emphasis on the quality and suitability of our cleaning tools and equipment. Our teams use professional equipment from recognized international brands, including Hako and Kärcher, selected according to the cleaning task, surface and site requirements.

By combining high-quality equipment with appropriate products and qualified personnel, we support efficient cleaning and consistent standards of work.

Our Working Approach

We begin by assessing the premises and identifying cleaning priorities. We then define the service scope, coordinate staffing and schedules, and select suitable equipment and methods for the surfaces involved.

Throughout the service, we maintain communication with client management, follow up on site observations and adjust priorities as operational needs change.
