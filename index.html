ABOUT BRIGHT

Who we are

Supporting businesses across Lebanon since 2005.

Bright Service Company SARL is a Lebanese company providing facility management and operational support services to businesses across diverse sectors. Established in 2005, we bring more than two decades of practical experience to maintaining commercial facilities and supporting daily business operations.

We combine qualified personnel, operational knowledge and close coordination with client management to deliver services tailored to each organization’s premises, schedules and priorities.

Our history

Since our establishment in 2005, we have developed our expertise through hands-on work in the Lebanese market. This experience has shaped our understanding of the challenges businesses face in maintaining their facilities, coordinating personnel and ensuring continuity of service.

Today, Bright Service brings together five complementary service areas, offering clients coordinated support for their facilities, employees and operations. We continue to build lasting working relationships through consistent service, clear communication and attention to each client’s requirements.

Our mission

To provide dependable facility and operational support services that help businesses maintain their premises, support their people and keep their daily operations running smoothly.

Our vision

To be a trusted long-term partner for businesses across Lebanon, recognized for professional teams, consistent service and practical solutions.

Our values

Reliability

Delivering agreed services consistently and following through on our commitments.

Professionalism

Maintaining respectful conduct, capable teams and clear standards of work.

Accountability

Taking responsibility for our work and addressing client observations.

Safety & care

Working with attention to people, property, equipment and the surrounding environment.

Partnership

Listening to clients and coordinating closely with their management teams.

Continuous improvement

Reviewing our methods and adapting to changing operational needs.

Our commitment

We approach every assignment with a clear understanding of its scope and the client’s expectations. Through organized teams, practical planning and regular follow-up, we work to deliver consistent service and build confidence in the support we provide.
